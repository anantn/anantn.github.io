[Gentoo](https://gentoo.org/) was my Linux distribution of choice for many years. I eventually waded into helping out by maintaining a few packages and writing a web-based editor for GuideXML, Gentoo's documentation format.

I owe a lot of my understanding of Linux to Gentoo. I would still recommend that anyone interested in the operating system install Gentoo at least once. You learn so much because the distribution doesn't hide the internals from you. On Gentoo you compile everything from source.

Over the years, I found myself with less free time, so the practicality of Ubuntu on the desktop and a MacBook Pro on the go eventually won out.
